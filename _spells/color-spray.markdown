**1st-level illusion**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of colorful sand)

**Duration**: Instantaneous

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 15-foot Cone originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the &amp;Reference[blinded apply=false] condition until the end of your next turn.
