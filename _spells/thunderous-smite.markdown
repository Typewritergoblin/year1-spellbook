**1st-level evocation**

**Casting Time**: 1 bonus action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V

**Duration**: Instantaneous

Your strike rings with thunder that is audible within 300 feet of you, and the target takes an extra 2d6 Thunder damage from the attack. Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from you and have the &amp;Reference[prone apply=false] condition.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. **The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
