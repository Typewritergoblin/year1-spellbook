**1st-level conjuration**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S, M (a sprig of mistletoe)

**Duration**: 24 hours

@UUID[Compendium.dnd-players-handbook.spells.Item.phbutlMagicalBer]{Ten berries} appear in your hand and are infused with magic for the duration. A creature can take a Bonus Action to eat one berry. Eating a berry restores 1 Hit Point, and the berry provides enough nourishment to sustain a creature for one day.

Uneaten berries disappear when the spell ends.
