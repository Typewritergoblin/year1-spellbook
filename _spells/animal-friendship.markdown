**1st-level enchantment**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 30 feet

**Components**: V, S, M (a morsel of food)

**Duration**: 24 hours

Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the &amp;Reference[charmed apply=false] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spells ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
