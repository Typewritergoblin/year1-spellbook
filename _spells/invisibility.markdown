**2nd-level illusion**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Touch

**Components**: V, S, M (an eyelash in gum arabic)

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour

A creature you touch has the &amp;Reference[invisible apply=false] condition until the spell ends. The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
