**8th-level transmutation**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Touch

**Components**: V, S, M

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 hour

A willing creature you touch turns to living metal for the duration. It can't accrue further Exhaustion levels; it gains Resistance to Bludgeoning, Fire, Piercing, and Slashing damage; and it gains Immunity to Poison damage and to the Paralyzed, Petrified, and Poisoned conditions, ending any of those it already has when the spell is cast.
