**Transmutation cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 30 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: 10 minutes

You create a magical effect within range. Choose the effect from the options below. If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time. While one or more of these effects is active, you gain a +1d4 to Performance checks.

**Accompaniment.** You enchant an existing musical instrument (not worn or carried by another creature) to play a simple melody of your choice, or you create a small ambient sound repeats within range for the duration. This could be a steady drumbeat, a melodic humming, a rhythmic bassline, or quiet strings.

**Choir.** You magically throw your voice to another point within range. It sounds just like your voice, but you can talk, sing, or otherwise make sounds with it. You can choose to throw your voice or have it echo your voice, chosen when you cast it.

**Costume Change.** You subtly alter the appearance of your nonmagical garments, shifting their color, causing them to gleam or glitter, or causing hems or capes to billow as if in a dramatic breeze. 

**Flair.** You can cause a harmless sensory burst or ambient effect, such as visual effects to follow the movements of your body, (glittering dust, colorful strands of light, smoke) or a burst of smoke, confetti, sparks, etc.
