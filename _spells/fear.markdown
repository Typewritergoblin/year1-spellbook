**3rd-level illusion**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V, S, M (a white feather)

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Each creature in a 30-foot Cone must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or drop whatever it is holding and have the &amp;Reference[frightened apply=false] condition for the duration.

A Frightened creature takes the &amp;Reference[dash] action and moves away from you by the safest route on each of its turns unless there is nowhere to move. If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn’t have line of sight to you, the creature makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the spell ends on that creature.
