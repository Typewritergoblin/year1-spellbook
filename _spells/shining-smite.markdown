**2nd-level transmutation**

**Casting Time**: 1 bonus action

**Range**: Self

**Components**: V

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage from the attack. Until the spell ends, the target sheds &amp;Reference[BrightLight] in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have Advantage, and it can’t benefit from the &amp;Reference[Invisible] condition.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
