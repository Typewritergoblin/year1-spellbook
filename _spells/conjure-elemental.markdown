**5th-level conjuration**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 60 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You conjure a Large, intangible spirit from the Elemental Planes that appears in an unoccupied space within range. Choose the spirit’s element, which determines its damage type: @UUID[Compendium.dnd-players-handbook.actors.Actor.phbsplConjuredEl]{air (Lightning)}, @UUID[Compendium.dnd-players-handbook.actors.Actor.phbConjuredEarth]{earth (Thunder)}, @UUID[Compendium.dnd-players-handbook.actors.Actor.phbConjuredFireE]{fire (Fire)}, or @UUID[Compendium.dnd-players-handbook.actors.Actor.phbConjuredWater]{water (Cold)}. The spirit lasts for the duration.

Whenever a creature you can see enters the spirit’s space or starts its turn within 5 feet of the spirit, you can force that creature to make a Dexterity saving throw if the spirit has no creature Restrained. On failed save, the target takes 8d8 damage of the spirit’s type, and the target has the &amp;Reference[Restrained apply=false] condition until the spell ends. At the start of each of its turns, the Restrained target repeats the save. On a failed save, the target takes 4d8 damage of the spirit’s type. On a successful save, the target isn’t Restrained by the spirit.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.
