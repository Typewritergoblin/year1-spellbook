**5th-level necromancy**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 60 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You lash a target in range with draining darkness. It makes a Dexterity save, taking 6d8 Necrotic damage on a failure (half on a success, which also ends the spell); on a failed save the spell persists, and each later turn you can spend a Bonus Action to deal 2d8 more Necrotic damage automatically. The spell ends if the target is ever outside the spell's range or it has Total Cover from you. Any time this spell damages the target, you regain Hit Points equal to half that damage.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The initial damage increases by 1d8 per slot level above 5.
