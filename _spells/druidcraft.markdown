**Transmutation cantrip**

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 30 feet

**Components**: V, S

**Duration**: Instantaneous to 1 hour.

Whispering to the spirits of nature, you create one of the following effects within range.

**Weather Sensor.** You create a Tiny, harmless sensory effect that predicts what the weather will be at your location for the next 24 hours. The effect might manifest as a golden orb for clear skies, a cloud for rain, falling snowflakes for snow, and so on. This effect persists for 1 round.

**Bloom.** You instantly make a flower blossom, a seed pod open, or a leaf bud bloom.

**Sensory Effect.** You create a harmless sensory effect, such as falling leaves, spectral dancing fairies, a gentle breeze, the sound of an animal, or the faint odor of skunk. The effect must fit in a 5-foot Cube.

**Fire Play.** You light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a campfire.

**Dowsing Rod.** Place a leaf or seed on a flat surface or your palm, and it will slightly tilt toward the nearest source of water or primal energies.

**Reclaim.** You command grass, dirt, or fallen foilage to swirl in a 5-foot cube, helping conceal non-magical tracks or disturbances and otherwise return the area to a more natural state.

**Bounded Glade.** A place of natural shelter can be protected by negotiating with the spirits. By walking the bounds of up a 30' radius over ten minutes, you can request the spirits make it more hospitable, reducing or increasing the temperature by around 10 degrees, protecting it slightly from wind, rain, or hail, drying the ground slightly. Survival checks within this bounded land gain a +1d4 bonus for 1 hour.
